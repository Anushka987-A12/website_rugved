Overview

The AI & Robotics division is responsible for everything that lets our vehicles perceive their surroundings and act on their own. From sensor fusion and path planning to swarm coordination, this division turns raw hardware signals into decisions the platform can execute in the field.

Focus areas

  • Autonomous navigation

    Real-time mapping, localisation, and obstacle avoidance that let the UGV navigate unstructured terrain without manual input.

  • Perception & vision

    Camera-based detection and recognition pipelines used for target identification and scene understanding.

  • Swarm coordination

    Parent-child architectures that let multiple UAVs share sensing, negotiate hand-offs, and cover more ground together.

  • Behaviour & planning

    Mission logic and decision-making layers that decide what the platform should do next given its objective and surroundings.
