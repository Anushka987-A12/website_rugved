Overview

The Mechanical division builds the chassis and structures that everything else mounts to. It covers vehicle design, suspension and drivetrain choices, payload integration, and the material and manufacturing decisions that determine how well the platform survives field conditions.

Focus areas

  • Chassis design

    All-terrain vehicle architecture designed around modularity and repairability.

  • Drivetrain & mobility

    Tracked and wheeled mobility solutions tuned for rough terrain and load capacity.

  • Payload integration

    Mounting and wiring solutions so sensors, cameras, and radar fit without rework.

  • Materials & durability

    Material selection and structural testing for weight, strength, and long service life.
