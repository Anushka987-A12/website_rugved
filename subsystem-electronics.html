Electronics

Sensors, microcontrollers, circuits, and system functionality — the hardware foundation of the platform.

← All subsystems

Overview

The Electronics division designs and builds the hardware that every other subsystem depends on. This includes sensor selection and wiring, microcontroller board design, power distribution, and the integration work that brings disparate modules together into one reliable system.

Focus areas

  • Sensor integration

    Selecting, wiring, and calibrating the sensing hardware that feeds data into the autonomy and research pipelines.

  • Microcontroller systems

    Firmware and board-level design for the embedded controllers that run our real-time control loops.

  • Circuit design

    Schematic design and prototyping for power, signal conditioning, and communication interfaces.

  • Radar & RF

    Ground-penetrating radar research integrated into the vehicle's sensing stack.
