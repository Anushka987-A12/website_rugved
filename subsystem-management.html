Management

Project tracking, resource allocation, coordination, and progress monitoring across every division.

← All subsystems

Overview

The Management division keeps the project on schedule and resourced properly. It maintains milestones, coordinates work between divisions, manages budgets and materials, and reports progress so leadership can make decisions with current information.

Focus areas

  • Project tracking

    Milestone planning and status tracking so every division knows its current commitments.

  • Resource allocation

    Budgets, components, tools, and lab time allocated where they create the most progress.

  • Cross-division coordination

    Keeping hardware, software, and research work aligned so integration does not stall.

  • Sponsor relations

    Managing partner and sponsor engagement, reporting, and event logistics.
