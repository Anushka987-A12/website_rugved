Overview

The AI Research division handles the thinking behind the build. This division studies sensing technologies, evaluates algorithms, and documents findings so the rest of the team can make informed choices instead of guesses.

Focus areas

  • Applied research

    Investigating sensing and perception methods to find approaches that suit our platforms and constraints.

  • Algorithm evaluation

    Benchmarking candidate algorithms against real data so we adopt what actually performs.

  • Data analysis

    Extracting useful signal from sensor recordings and field observations.

  • Documentation

    Recording designs, results, and decisions so knowledge stays with the project rather than in one person's head.
